Before the drive

  1. Download and install the APK on the S23+. Grant Nearby devices and notification permissions.
  2. Use Test spoken instructions. Check the phone/car audio volume and that an English TTS voice is installed. The app uses Android text-to-speech; voice quality depends on your installed engine.
  3. Optionally run Run demo capture (no car), then stop and export to check the file workflow. Demo data is synthetic. A guided desk demo waits for simulated motion after its parked section; it is not a real drive.
  4. While parked, connect the OBD adapter and pair it in Android Bluetooth settings. Refresh paired devices and select it.
  5. Choose Magic or Guided. For Guided, remain parked in P with parking brake on and engine idling, then tap Start selected vehicle capture and confirm the parked setup. Wait for spoken instructions.

How to follow the guided route

The sequence assumes a suitable road with a 90 km/h limit. Actual posted limits, traffic, visibility, and comfortable engine RPM always take priority. The 60/70 targets and every stronger input or gear request are optional. There is no sustained parked throttle test, emergency-stop requirement, or multi-gear downshift.

Drive continuously and follow only the actions that fit the road. You do not need to touch the phone. Prompts are timestamped as instructions, not proof that you performed the action. We can compare the actual telemetry afterward even when your timing differs.

Moving-stage timers pause at stops or when speed data is stale. A stronger-input window waits for a naturally lower pace and skips itself after 75 seconds if no suitable opportunity appears. Do not slow just to activate it. Passenger/parked controls can pause, repeat or skip instructions.

Full spoken sequence · 21 stages
  1. parked baseline25 seconds of eligible timeKeep the car parked in P, parking brake on, engine idling. Leave the accelerator alone. Rest your foot off the foot brake for a baseline.
  2. parked light brake25 seconds of eligible timeWhile still parked, press the foot brake lightly for two seconds, then release it fully. Repeat once. Leave the accelerator alone.
  3. parked firm brake30 seconds of eligible timeStill parked in P, press the foot brake more firmly but comfortably for two seconds, then release. Then make two light taps followed by one firmer press. No accelerator input.
  4. depart 6075 seconds of eligible timeBegin your normal journey when ready. If the road permits, build speed smoothly toward sixty kilometers per hour with light accelerator and comfortable low revs. Do not lug the engine, force a gear, or chase the target.
  5. cruise 6055 seconds of eligible timeOn the assumed ninety limit road, if appropriate, settle near sixty and enable cruise at your current speed. Lift your foot off the accelerator and let cruise hold it. Skip cruise if conditions do not suit.
  6. cruise 7065 seconds of eligible timeIf cruise is active and the road is clear, gradually raise its set speed toward seventy using the cruise buttons. Let the speed settle. Follow any lower posted limit instead; seventy is not required.
  7. brake cancel45 seconds of eligible timeAt the next appropriate slowdown, brake gently to cancel cruise, then continue normally. Do not create an unnecessary stop. This is the brake cancellation observation window.
  8. soft gas40 seconds of eligible timeWith cruise off, accelerate softly from your current pace using a light pedal, then ease off and coast naturally. This is about gentle pedal depth, not reaching a speed.
  9. progressive gas40 seconds of eligible timeWhen appropriate, gradually press the accelerator from light toward moderate for a few seconds, then ease back and release. Let the car coast. Choose your own timing and stay below the limit.
  10. brief full gas40 seconds of eligible timeOptional stronger input, only with clear road and plenty of speed headroom. Smoothly press fully for about one second, ease back toward moderate pedal, then lift off. Skip this entirely if unsuitable. Do not sustain full throttle or chase a target speed.
  11. light brake35 seconds of eligible timeAfter coasting, use a light brake application when you next naturally need to slow. Release it and return to ordinary driving. There is no need to stop.
  12. alternate gas45 seconds of eligible timeIf appropriate, build accelerator pressure smoothly from light to moderate, ease it back to light, then release and coast. A brief firmer input is optional. Do not pump the pedals or alternate brake and accelerator together.
  13. firmer brake40 seconds of eligible timeCoast first. At a naturally needed slowdown, you may brake more firmly than before, then release and settle. No emergency stop, target deceleration, or immediate full acceleration is requested.
  14. cruise resume60 seconds of eligible timeOptional cruise check. If its remembered speed is suitable, resume once and let it settle. Otherwise skip it. Then cancel with the cancel button when appropriate and continue with cruise off.
  15. manual prepare75 seconds of eligible timeThe next section is optional manual shifting with cruise off. Settle at a steady legal pace, up to around ninety only if appropriate, leaving margin to the speed limit. Let D choose seventh if it naturally does. Do not accelerate just to reach seventh.
  16. manual seventh30 seconds of eligible timeIf you are steady in seventh and comfortable, select manual mode and keep seventh for a moment. If not in seventh, skip the requested gear sequence and drive normally.
  17. manual sixth25 seconds of eligible timeIf suitable at your current speed and revs, make one adjacent shift from seventh to sixth. Maintain a comfortable legal pace and let it settle. Skip any shift that would require high revs or excessive speed.
  18. manual fifth25 seconds of eligible timeIf sixth is settled and revs allow, make one adjacent shift to fifth, then hold a steady comfortable pace. Do not force the downshift. Skip it if unsuitable.
  19. manual back sixth25 seconds of eligible timeWhen appropriate, shift from fifth back to sixth and let it settle. Keep speed and traffic conditions ahead of the gear request.
  20. manual back seventh25 seconds of eligible timeWhen appropriate, make the next adjacent upshift to seventh and let it settle. Then return to normal D when comfortable. No multi gear downshift is requested.
  21. normal finish60 seconds of eligible timeContinue your normal drive. The guided sequence is nearly finished. Recording will continue after the final instruction. Keep driving until you can park, then stop and export the session.

After each capture

  1. Keep driving normally after the final instruction, until you can pull over and park.
  2. Tap Stop and save. Use Save a session to Files or Share a saved session.
  3. Send the .jsonl file privately in the project chat. Ideally send one ordinary Magic capture and one Guided capture; they may be separate drives.

What changed

  • 15 ms serial turnaround instead of the previous fixed 100 ms pause.
  • Priority scheduling, fewer redundant addressing commands, finite discovery, and measured per-request cadence in the log. Desired rates are not guarantees.
  • Raw-byte logging remains intact. Buffered writes sync roughly every half-second; markers and a normal stop sync immediately. A sudden kill may lose the recent unsynced tail.
  • Guided instructions, actual speech callbacks, pauses, skips, and completion are logged separately. Capture continues after guide completion.

Build, protocol/scheduler/guide tests, desktop tools, and Android emulator capture tests are checked. Physical-adapter throughput and audible TTS routing on your S23+ still need verification. A diagnostic reply does not validate its sensor interpretation.

Recordings can contain VIN and driving telemetry. Keep them out of the public repository. The app uses read-only ECU requests; it does not clear faults or run actuator tests. Source & technical documentation.
